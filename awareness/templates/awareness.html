Cyber Awareness

Stay informed and protect yourself from online threats.

Top Safety Tips

  • Do not click suspicious links or download unknown attachments.
  • Use strong, unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Be careful with personal information on public platforms.
  • Report suspicious accounts, messages, or behavior immediately.

Common Threats

Phishing, fake profiles, cyberbullying, and scam messages are common issues. Awareness helps you respond quickly and safely.
